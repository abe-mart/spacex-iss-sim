BEGINNER / FLIGHT SETUP

AI CAPCOM

You fly the capsule while AI CAPCOM, an AI flight controller, reads your telemetry and talks you in by radio. It tells you which keys to press, and you can ask it questions during the flight. Open the simulator, enter a crew name, and press LAUNCH FLIGHT. No installation is required.

Controls: W / S move up and down, A / D move left and right, and E / Q move forward and backward. The arrow keys control pitch and yaw, and the comma and period keys control roll. The on-screen buttons work as well.

AI CAPCOM uses Claude Haiku by default. You can choose a different Claude model in the flight panel, or choose None to fly without AI. The leaderboard shows whether each flight used AI CAPCOM.

Docking and scoring

Position is relative to the docking port: x is the distance along the docking axis, y is the sideways offset, and z is the vertical offset. Units are meters, meters per second, and degrees. The capsule docks when the range is below 0.2 m, roll, pitch, and yaw are each within 0.2°, and each velocity component is at or below 0.24 m/s. Contact with the station anywhere else, or drifting more than 500 m away, ends the flight.

Only successful real-time flights appear on the leaderboard. The lowest simulation time wins, and fewer thruster pulses break ties. Keep the simulator tab visible during a flight, because browsers slow down hidden tabs.

Results are reported by the browser and reviewed by event staff.
